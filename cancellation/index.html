Customer policy

Cancellation policy.

Cancellations are handled by email. Phone calls are not accepted as cancellation requests.

How to cancel

Send the cancellation request from the same email address used for the reservation or associated credit-card authorization.

Cancellation@GreenRide.net

Refund timing

A full refund is available when the cancellation email is received at least 4 hours before pickup during Green Ride business hours, subject to the complete terms and any route-specific notice requirements.

No refund is available for services already rendered, qualifying late cancellations, or no-shows as described in the full terms.
