Pricing explained

How flat rate rides work.

The difference between a published upfront base rate and a running metered fare.

The simple version

Published flat / upfront base rate

The base amount for a listed route is shown before travel.

Metered fare

The base fare changes with time and/or distance while the trip is underway.

Additional charges

Gratuity, tolls, parking, waiting, stops, fuel surcharges or special-event pricing may still apply.

Why upfront pricing helps

  • You can review the listed base rate before travel.
  • You can compare routes before booking.
  • You have a published reference instead of relying only on a verbal estimate.
  • You can call and confirm applicable extras before the trip.
