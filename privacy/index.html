Privacy & SMS.

How Green Ride handles customer information and trip-related text-message consent.

Information we collect

Green Ride may collect identifiers such as name, email, phone, pickup and destination information, payment-related information, booking details, and ordinary website usage information needed to provide and improve service.

How information is used

Information is used to provide transportation, process payments, communicate about trips, respond to customer requests, improve the service, and meet legal obligations.

SMS consent

Trip-related SMS messages require consent. Phone numbers collected for SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Reply STOP to opt out. Message and data rates may apply.
