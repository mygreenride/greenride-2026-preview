Terms & Conditions.

The operating terms for Green Ride transportation, cancellations, no-shows, fees, passenger conduct and liability.

Guarantees and travel time

No refunds are available for services rendered. Airlines generally recommend arriving well before departure. Traffic, weather, road conditions, airport conditions and other circumstances beyond Green Ride's control can affect travel time; exact arrival times are not guaranteed.

Airport curbside pickup

For San Diego International Airport curbside service, call when the flight lands and call again when outside at the curb with the nearest zone sign. Vehicles cannot remain waiting at the curb except while loading or unloading.

Cancellation

Cancellations are accepted by email and are valid once acknowledged by Green Ride. San Diego-area service generally requires at least 4 hours' notice; other regions may require longer notice. Late cancellations may be charged under the applicable service minimum.

No-show

If you cannot locate the driver, call Green Ride before leaving the pickup location. Airport no-show rules may apply after 60 minutes for domestic arrivals or 75 minutes for international arrivals when the passenger has not met or contacted the driver.

Waiting time and stops

Point-to-point reservations include a 5-minute grace period. Additional waiting may be charged at $1 per minute. Stops and off-route service may carry additional or hourly charges.

Additional charges

Tolls, parking, airport fees and similar out-of-pocket charges may be additional. Published rates may exclude a 20% driver gratuity and applicable fuel surcharge.

Baggage, property and cleaning

Personal property is transported at the passenger's risk. Excessive cleaning or damage may result in additional charges, including charges for spills, smoke or burns, vomit, broken items, repairs and vehicle downtime.

Passenger conduct and prohibited items

Passengers must not interfere with the driver or vehicle equipment. Vehicles are non-smoking. Weapons, explosives, fireworks, flammable, combustible or toxic materials are prohibited unless specifically permitted by law and approved where applicable.

Liability and force majeure

Green Ride is not responsible for certain indirect or consequential losses or delays caused by events outside its reasonable control, subject to applicable law. The complete liability limitations in Green Ride's service agreement apply.

Governing law and disputes

California law governs the terms. The existing Green Ride terms provide for dispute resolution through the American Arbitration Association and specify San Diego County, California, subject to the arbitration provisions and applicable law.

Agreement

By booking Green Ride transportation, the customer acknowledges the applicable rates, policies and terms. Contact Green Ride with questions before booking.
